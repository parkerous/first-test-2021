🎓 NCAA Men's D1 Volleyball Stats

The stat categories the NCAA tracks for Division I men's volleyball — what each one means, and why it matters for your game in Binsu. Every card opens the live official leaderboard on NCAA.com.

💥

Kills Per Set

Attacks that end the rally. The headline stat for every outside hitter and opposite — finish rate wins games.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🎯

Hitting Percentage

(Kills − errors) ÷ attempts. The efficiency stat — swinging hard means nothing if you're hitting into the block.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🤝

Assists Per Set

The setter's stat. Great setters turn bad passes into kills — assists measure how often the offense runs through you.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🚀

Aces Per Set

Serves that score untouched or force an unplayable pass. Pressure from the service line breaks an offense before it starts.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🧱

Blocks Per Set

Solo + assisted stuffs at the net. The middle blocker's headline number — a good block also shapes every dig behind it.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🛡️

Digs Per Set

Attacks kept off the floor. The libero's stat — defense turns the other team's best swing into your transition point.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🚫

Opponent Hitting %

How badly you make the other team hit. The best defensive teams hold opponents under .200 — team defense in one number.

Live NCAA leaderboard → 🏆
